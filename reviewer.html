One entry, one virtue. Your answer is a label.

VirtuOS records how good people act across traditions. Each entry has four parts: the situation, what the wise person did or said, why it worked, and the tempting wrong answer.

Your job is one judgment. Does this entry teach the virtue named below as its lesson? Record whether it holds. Approval is someone else's job.

If your traditions are outside this entry's lineage, your label counts toward that virtue's reach. If you share a lineage with it, we store your label and it does not count. We hide the tradition from you and run that check on the server.

A virtue enters the shared core when five lineages reach it and reviewers outside each lineage agree. Nothing is in the core today.
