## VirtuOS — Pilot Scope (1 page)

**Title:** Reciprocity pilot — external wisdom eval, 6 weeks

**Objective:** Produce one citable, provenance-backed scorecard for reciprocity that a lab can cite in a system card. Not a data purchase — the corpus is CC BY-SA.

**Virtue:** `reciprocity` (6 roots raw in `virtue_reach_raw.csv`; 0 reviewed — the hypothesis is that outside reviewers will sustain ≥5).

**Corpus:** 100 strength-3 entries, preregistered in `prereg/reciprocity.json` (lineages + sources named before collection, P1-P3).

**Reviewers needed from the lab (2):**
- `reviewer_virtue` role, `tradition_ids[]` set, outside lineages vs entry roots (R1), never author (R2)
- Labels are the evidence. Your reviewers' `agrees/notes` are the deliverable; no entry edits.

**Timeline:** 6 weeks
- Wk 1: reviewer onboarding + socialization set (20 items)
- Wk 2–5: blind labeling, 10% shared subsample for κ
- Wk 6: freeze + scorecard

**Deliverables (to lab):**
1. Scorecard: model performance on reciprocity scenarios (disguised, from verified entries)
2. Method note: tree v1 + codebook v0.5 versions, κ on shared subsample, `reviewer_unavailable` list
3. Reproducible pack: entry CSVs + labels + `scripts/verify-reach` log (any stranger can recompute)

**Deliverables (to VirtuOS):**
- 200 outside labels (100 entries × 2 reviewers)
- κ measurement (shared subsample only; >15 shared items for stability)
- Evidence whether reciprocity sustains 5 lineages under R1/R2

**What this does NOT include:**
- No judge model. No synthesis across virtues. No marketing claims — empty core remains possible and would be reported.
- No licence to redistribute `research_only` (GRETIL) or `unclear` entries. Release is CC BY-SA 4.0.

**Success criteria (both sides sign before start):**
- ≥80% of 100 entries get 2 qualifying outside labels
- κ reported (substantial = eligible for core; below = reported as below)
- Scorecard published with methods, even if results are unflattering

**Cost to lab:** reviewer time only. No data fee. If pilot validates, next step is custodian membership (dues, not a licence).

**Why now:** reciprocity is your strongest raw signal (6 roots). If it survives outside review, you have your first defensible reference. If it doesn't, you've still bought the most valuable thing: knowing.
