## For philanthropy — "Fund the measurement, not the marketing"

> The field's best evaluators know models can detect when they're being tested. VirtuOS is the unannounced inspection — disguised wisdom scenarios, with human reviewers from the traditions in the corpus.

**Why this is fundable now:**
- The standard is a method, not a product. Our first release will have an *empty core* — and that honesty is what makes it trustworthy.
- Reviewer pool is the bottleneck. Your money buys independent judgment (R1 outside lineage, R2 not author), not more generation.
- 1,523 live entries. 985 with original text. 538 english-only. 11 Sanskrit entries, 0 verified. The gap is Queue A translation verification.

**The ask:** Seed grant $10k–$50k for the first reviewer cohort (recruitment, calibration, translation verification for Sanskrit/Arabic/Coptic). At $650k endowment (≈4% draw) the core sustains 10 years.

**What you get:** Named custodian board seat option (non-operating), quarterly observer logs (builder claims vs second-pass recounts), and a public scoreboard whether or not models do well.

**What we don't do:** We don't grade virtues for you. No recommendations to change data. The audit is a second pass — it reports, humans decide.

**Deliverable:** One frozen DOI release (`virtuos_v1.0`) with provenance, within 12 months of funding.

Reach, not moral truth. We measure independent attestation, we don't claim correctness.
