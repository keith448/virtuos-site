# Reviewer targets — first cohort

Six reviewers for reciprocity. One is onboarded. This list is for the next two
to four.

How to use it:

1. Take one root a week. Pick two units.
2. Find a named person on the unit page: a lecturer, postdoc, PhD candidate, or
   affiliated clergy. People two to ten years into the field answer email, and
   the pay matters to them. Save the senior professors for adjudication.
3. Send the invitation from `docs/reviewer-brief.md`. One follow-up after ten
   days. Then stop.
4. Log it in `docs/reviewer-outreach.csv`.

Do not scrape emails. Use the published department or center contact and ask the
office to forward. A forwarded note from a chair gets read.

## Referral ask

When someone declines, ask one question:

> Is there a colleague or doctoral student who reads [language] and might take
> two paid hours?

Referrals convert better than cold sends and cost one sentence.

## Funnel and cost

- Forty sends, about ten replies, six calls, four onboarded. Two touches each.
- Sixty labels is eight reviewer-hours in total, US$500 to US$1,000.
- Prefer postdocs and doctoral candidates for the first batch. Keep two senior
  scholars warm for adjudication.

## Where not to go

- General crowdwork platforms. The work is a cultural judgment, and V1/V2 need
  formation in a tradition or its language.
- Scraped email lists.
- One tradition's institution for every root. A single-root cohort cannot satisfy
  R1 for its own entries.
