## For university — "A dissemination artifact, not an app"

> Cross-tradition values are typically asserted. VirtuOS counts independent attestation — with lineage trees, rejection logs, and named editions — so the claim becomes checkable.

**What you get (deliverable, not dependency):**
- Methods paper: codebook + tree v1 + mercy 0 finding + κ report. Descriptive, not advocacy.
- Frozen release: JSONL + `virtue_reach.csv` + tree + codebook, Apache-2.0 code / CC BY-SA data, LOCKSS archived. Survives Supabase.
- Reviewer network design: blind UI, shared subsample κ, `reviewer_unavailable` honesty.

**The ask (Horizon / NSF / internal seed):** $100k–$300k over 24 months for Queue A translation verification — the only queue that needs your graduates. Our Sanskrit/Pali/Arabic/Coptic language capacity is the bottleneck; your labs are the natural reviewers (R3: two readers, one from tradition).

**Why us:** Most ethics datasets are single-lab, single-language, single-approval. This one is built to be re-run by strangers with the same numbers. That's a publishable method, not a prototype.

**Cocreation path:** Your folklorists / theologians / film scholars sit on Queue B (discipline enum). We give you `tradition_ids[]` and a calibration set. You give us the outside judgment the codebook requires.

Reach, not moral truth. We measure independent attestation, we don't claim correctness.
